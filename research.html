Featured Research Projects

Enhancing Academic Writing and Digital Literacy with an AI Coach

Digital Learning Lab, University of California, Irvine · June 2026–Present

I harmonize CELL data across UCI, CSUF, and NOCCCD, covering approximately 45 instructors and more than 2,700 students across multiple terms. My work includes pre/post matching, scoring, coding, and missing-data standardization. I conduct longitudinal and subgroup analyses of PapyrusAI adoption, experiences, and learning outcomes, and lead manuscript development based on project findings.

Explainable Learning Assessment via Event-based Knowledge Graphs

National Natural Science Foundation of China · January 2021–December 2024

I contributed to proposal preparation and the technical roadmap, served as a core developer for the Multi-layer Event Knowledge Graph module, and collaborated on the design and validation of a GNN-based diagnostic model.

AI-based Evaluation and Precision Support for Teachers’ Instructional Competence in Rural Areas

National Key R&D Program of China · January 2023–December 2026

I coordinated Sub-project 3 execution in collaboration with Beijing Normal University and refined the Knowledge Forest technical roadmap. My technical contributions included deep geometric GCN methods for semantic representations and a deep reinforcement learning framework for teaching path generation.

Education Ethics and Risk Mitigation in the ChatGPT Era

Zhejiang Provincial Social Sciences Project · January 2024–December 2025

I reviewed generative AI ethics research and supported the development of an ethical framework. I also conducted empirical analyses of algorithmic bias in large language models in educational contexts and proposed technical strategies for risk mitigation and fairness auditing.
